About Gordian — The U.S.‑Based Company

Gordian is a U.S.‑headquartered company (based in Greenville, South Carolina, USA) that specializes in Building Intelligence™ solutions — meaning it provides data, software and services that help organizations plan, estimate, budget, procure, build, and maintain their capital‑intensive facilities and infrastructure.

Gordian helps organizations simplify construction planning and procurement. With reliable data, expert tools, and industry best practices, we help you reduce costs, save time, and deliver projects efficiently.

Our Solutions

📊 Data & Insights

Gordian produces the RSMeans™ Data, which is North America’s leading construction cost and estimating database.

🛠️ Software & Tech

Gordian develops tools that help clients estimate costs, benchmark performance, model projects, and optimize facility spending.

📋 Services & Expertise

Gordian’s experts support planning, procurement strategies, capital budgeting, and lifecycle cost analysis.

🤝 Industry Role

Gordian is known for creating Job Order Contracting (JOC) and setting standards in construction cost data.

Typical Functions & Solutions at Gordian.

Gordian’s work is often organized around core functions that support its business mission:

🔹 Estimating & Cost Data

One of Gordian’s core offerings is cost estimating and forecasting — providing accurate construction cost data (RSMeans) that teams use to budget projects and make planning decisions.

🔹 Planning & Capital Strategy

Teams use tools and expertise to help clients prioritize projects, assess risk, and plan capital investments over time.

🔹 Procurement Solutions

Gordian’s procurement solutions — like JOC — help public agencies and organizations streamline how they contract for construction services, often reducing time and cost.

🔹Asset Optimization

Ongoing optimization support helps clients monitor space, performance metrics, and operational costs across their portfolio of facilities.
